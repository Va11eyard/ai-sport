Decision quality10

Risks and what would change

What could go wrong

  • Gueye's passing. A safe short passer, not a creative progressor. Keep Drinkwater as the passer.
  • Cook's step up. Bournemouth defended deep. Pair him with a progressive partner.
  • Ighalo's outlier. One strong season at 27. Use him as rotation, not a long contract.
  • Adaptation. The 4-4-2 pressing triggers need pre-season work.

What would change the answer

  • Budget minus 20%: the recommended plan still fits at EUR 24m.
  • Budget minus 40%: the base plan still fits at EUR 27m.
  • If the board wants a higher ceiling, Cook can become van Dijk for EUR 33m total.
  • If the board wants more passing, Gueye can become Coquelin for EUR 27m.

Limitations: one league of event data, market value as a fee proxy, retrospective outcomes, noisy residuals.
